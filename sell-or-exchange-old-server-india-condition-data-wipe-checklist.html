How to Sell or Exchange an Old Server in India: Condition, Data Wipe and Paperwork Checklist

To sell or exchange an old server in India, do four things in this order: identify the machine exactly (brand, model, serial number and configuration), grade its condition honestly, sanitise every drive and reset the management settings, and then choose between a cash sale and an exchange against new hardware. This checklist covers each step, what usually decides the value, and how Serverwale’s exchange of an old server works. For the wider decommissioning picture, read our server decommissioning and data wiping guide.

Step 1: identify the server and record the serial numbers

Buyers value a server by brand, model, generation and what is inside it, so write down the exact model, the CPU, RAM, drives, RAID card and power supplies, and read the serial number from the label and the management controller. A serial lookup also tells the buyer the machine’s age; see how to check server warranty by serial number for Dell, HPE, Lenovo, IBM, Cisco and Supermicro.

Step 3: sanitise the data before the machine leaves

NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2, Guidelines for Media Sanitization (final in September 2025; its FAQ was published 16 July 2026), describes three methods: clear, purge and destroy. Practical points from NIST’s FAQ:

  • Sanitise the whole device. NIST says sanitising the entire storage device is always preferred because of overprovisioning and bad-block remapping.
  • Multi-pass overwriting is unnecessary. For modern storage, NIST says legacy multi-pass practices give very little protection and can shorten flash media life; clear typically means a single overwrite pass or a manufacturer factory reset.
  • Match the method to how sensitive the data is and where the drive is going. NIST’s decision flow uses both, so use a stronger method when drives leave your organisation.
  • Verify the result. NIST separates verification (checking the technique completed without errors) from validation (deciding the outcome is acceptable for the data).

Also reset the management controller (iDRAC, iLO, XClarity or IPMI) and BIOS passwords, clear RAID configurations as needed, and remove any SD or USB boot media. Ask any buyer for their wipe method and a written confirmation. Serverwale’s exchange page states that it provides a certified data wipe; confirm what the certificate covers before you rely on it.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sell an old server in India?

Identify the exact model and serial number, grade the condition honestly, sanitise all drives and reset the management settings, then get a written valuation from a dealer or choose an exchange against new hardware. Keep the original CPU, RAM, power supplies and drives together, because missing parts lower the value.

What affects the value of an old server?

Brand, model and generation, whether it powers on and works, whether all components are present, and physical condition. Serverwale's exchange page states that the brand and condition decide the discount, from up to 20% for excellent condition down to up to 5% for older, worn servers.

How should I wipe a server before selling it?

Follow NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2: sanitise the whole storage device using clear, purge or destroy as the data sensitivity and destination require, and verify the result. NIST says multi-pass overwriting is unnecessary for modern storage; clear is typically one overwrite pass or a manufacturer factory reset.

Can I exchange an old server for a refurbished one at Serverwale?

Is the exchange discount fixed?

No. The percentages are the maximum for each condition band and are confirmed after physical inspection, subject to availability of the model you want.
